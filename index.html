Designed for everyday browsing

Adjust everything from the look to the search to fit your habits.

Dark and light mode

Pick your own accent color. The interface, home page and error pages change together, and tabs can move to the left side.

Choose your search engine

Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing and Yahoo are supported. The address bar and home search both use the engine you pick.

Home shortcuts and greeting

Add your favorite sites as home shortcuts, give each one a color, and write your own greeting.

Local file browsing

Type C: or D: in the address bar to browse drives and folders, and search the files in the current folder as you type.

PDF and saved pages

Press Ctrl+O to open local PDF, .html or .mhtml files in a tab.

Ad block and tabs

Ad block can be turned on or off. Tabs shrink as you open more. Ctrl+W closes a tab and Ctrl+R reloads the page.

Customize Mochi

Click the Mochi robot on the home page to pick a pet and a costume for it.

Full screen and zoom

Videos and pages that ask for full screen switch automatically. Press Esc or F11 to leave. Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- zoom the page.

Setup steps

Three steps to install and start using Mochi Browser.

1

Download the installer

Go to the download page and get the latest MochiBrowser-Setup.exe.

2

Run and install

Double-click the installer and follow the steps. You can choose whether to create a desktop shortcut.

3

Start customizing

Open Mochi Browser and click the Mochi robot on the home page to get started.

Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”?

This is a normal SmartScreen warning for new programs. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Uninstall

Go to Settings → Apps, find Mochi Browser and choose Uninstall.

System requirements: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Where is my browsing data stored?

Settings, bookmarks, history and site data are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MochiBrowser on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Does Mochi Browser collect my data?

Mochi Browser has no account system and no telemetry. The search engine and websites you use handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Can I clear my browsing history?

Yes. Use the three-dot menu to clear browsing data, or turn on “Clear history on exit” in General settings.

Google shows a CAPTCHA. What do I do?

Complete the verification on the page. Mochi will not replace the verification page with an error screen. If Google still won't load, try DuckDuckGo or Bing.

Why can't I copy and paste on a page?

Make sure the cursor is in the address bar or on the page content, then use Edit in the three-dot menu. If it still doesn't work, report it on GitHub.
